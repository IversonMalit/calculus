OUR WORK

Activities & Projects

A collection of the activities and projects we completed throughout the year.

01

Infomercial

We created a video explaining why students should care about Calculus and how it can be useful in their studies and future careers.

Reflection

This activity helped us understand that Calculus is more than just solving equations. We learned how to explain its importance in a simple and engaging way.

02

Calculus Poster

A visual design project illustrating Calculus principles through engaging graphics, concepts, and themes.

Reflection

Creating the poster allowed us to present Calculus concepts in a more creative way. It helped us see that mathematical ideas can also be communicated through visual design.

03

Function Operation

An activity focused on performing different operations involving functions.

Reflection

This activity strengthened my understanding of how functions can be combined and manipulated. It also helped me become more careful when following each step in solving problems.

04

Limit

An activity focused on understanding and solving problems involving limits.

Reflection

Learning about limits helped me understand an important foundation of Calculus. I learned how values can approach a certain point even when the function itself may behave differently.

05

Differentiation Rule

An activity involving the basic rules used in differentiation.

Reflection

This activity helped me understand the basic rules of differentiation and when to apply them. Practicing different problems made the process easier to follow.

06

Product, Quotient & Exponent Rule

An activity covering the Product Rule, Quotient Rule, and Exponent Rule in differentiation.

Reflection

This activity taught me that different types of functions require different differentiation rules. It improved my ability to identify which rule should be used when solving a problem.

07

Chain Rule

An activity focused on using the Chain Rule to differentiate composite functions.

Reflection

The Chain Rule helped me understand how functions can be connected within one another. Although it was challenging at first, repeated practice helped me understand the process better.

ASSESSMENTS

Examinations

A compilation of our major examinations throughout the year.

01

Prelim Examination

The Prelim examination tested our understanding of the concepts discussed during the first part of the course.

Reflection

The Prelim examination helped me identify the areas of Calculus that I understood and the topics that still needed more practice. It also taught me to manage my time when solving problems.

02

Midterm Examination

The Midterm examination allowed us to apply the concepts and problem-solving skills we developed throughout the first half of the course.

Reflection

The Midterm examination showed me how the different Calculus concepts we learned could be applied to actual problems. It encouraged me to review more carefully and learn from my mistakes.

03

Final Examination

The Final examination brought together the concepts we learned throughout the course and challenged us to apply them independently.

Reflection

The Final examination was a reflection of everything we learned throughout the year. It allowed me to apply the different rules and concepts I practiced and showed me how much I had improved.

LOOKING BACK

Overall Reflection

Throughout the year, Calculus challenged me to improve not only my mathematical skills but also my patience and problem-solving abilities. The different activities helped me understand concepts such as functions, limits, and differentiation step by step.

Some topics were difficult at first, but practicing the problems and learning from my mistakes helped me improve. Looking back at all the activities and examinations, I can see how much I learned throughout the course.
